## Brand & Style

This design system serves Indonesian senior high school students (classes XI–XII) navigating the pivot from secondary school to tertiary education, vocational training, or immediate careers. The emotional tone is grounded in the guiding philosophy: *"Pelan-pelan, yang penting mulai"* (Take it slow, what matters is beginning). The platform rejects high-stress corporate evaluation, anxiety-inducing percentile meters, and rigid psychometric testing in favor of a warm, structured, and editorial journey.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism:** The design merges bookish, warm paper-inspired surfaces with crisp modern digital ergonomics.
- **70-20-10 Color Balance:** 70% soft neutrals (Ivory canvas and pure white cards), 20% functional domain pastels for content chunking, and 10% purposeful deep maroon for interactive focal points.
- **Asymmetric Modular Rhythm:** Content lives in cleanly partitioned, rounded containers that mimic high-grade student workbooks and modern planner journals rather than corporate dashboards.
- **Natural Indonesian Tone:** Interface language is conversational, supportive, and unpretentious Bahasa Indonesia without clinical HR jargon.

## Colors

The palette establishes clarity without visual exhaustion. The default mode is permanently **light**, anchored by `#F7F3ED` (Ivory canvas) to evoke natural stationery and decrease eye strain during long self-reflection sessions.

### Functional Domains (Pastels)
Each pillar of student decision-making possesses a designated pastel identity:
- **Kenali Diriku (Self-Reflection):** `#F6E6EA` (Blush)
- **Peta Jalur (Pathways & Exploration):** `#E7EEE7` (Sage)
- **Timbang Pilihan (Comparison & Trade-offs):** `#EEE9F6` (Lavender)
- **Rencana Langkahku (Action Planning):** `#F9E7DB` (Peach)
- **Perhatian (Attention / Callout):** `#FFF0D5` (Butter)

### Contrast & State Rules
- **Interactive Maroon:** `#6E2035` is strictly reserved for primary actions, active navigational indicators, and critical links. Hover and active states transition to `#57172A`.
- **Text Readability:** All typography on pastels and ivory must use `#241A20` (Text Primary, delivering over 10:1 contrast) or `#6D6268` (Text Secondary, >5:1 contrast). Light or white text is strictly prohibited over pastel containers.
- **Focus Rings:** Accessibility keyboard focus is defined by a mandatory `3px solid #6E2035` ring with `3px` offset.
- **Pastel Rule:** Never combine all pastel tones within a single card; limit card tints to their functional domain.

## Typography

The platform utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels, taking advantage of its open counters, contemporary geometric structure, and humanistic warmth.

### Type Hierarchy Principles
- **Display & Headings:** Bold weights (`700`) anchor major screen titles. Line heights are calibrated tight enough for punchy Indonesian syntax without vertical collisions.
- **Fixed Reading Baseline:** Desktop and mobile viewport sizes share identical sizing for `body-default` (16px / 1.6 line-height) and `headline-card` (20px) to guarantee consistent cognitive comfort and eliminate jumpy text reflows.
- **Form Labels & Metadata:** Set at 14px with distinct weights (`600` for input labels to establish boundary definition; `400` for secondary metadata and auxiliary guidance).

## Layout & Spacing

### Desktop Layout Framework
- **Canvas Reference:** 1440px wide viewport.
- **Max Grid Container:** 1200px centered canvas (`margin: 0 auto`).
- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column fluid grid with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and minimum 32px outer canvas margins.
- **Card Spacing:** Standard interior container padding is set between 20px and 24px (`1.25rem` to `1.5rem`).

### Mobile Adaptation (<768px)
- **Grid:** Shifts to a 4-column fluid layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters.
- **Canvas Margins:** Safe horizontal margins scale to 16px–20px (`1rem`–`1.25rem`).
- **Touch Ergonomics:** Interactive targets maintain an absolute minimum touch zone of `44px x 44px`. Primary CTAs maintain a vertical height of `48px`.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids fake 3D depth, heavy drop shadows, and blurry frosted glass in favor of an **editorial planar** approach.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Surface (Level 0):** `#F7F3ED` (Ivory), creating a tactile, unprinted paper base.
2. **Component Cards (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` (White) or tinted functional pastels (`#F6E6EA`, `#E7EEE7`, `#EEE9F6`, `#F9E7DB`). Structured depth is achieved solely via surface color contrast and a subtle `1px solid #E7DEE2` border line. No drop shadow is used on standard cards.
3. **Elevated Drawers & Flyouts (Level 2):** Contextual detail side-drawers and dropdown menus utilize a minimal, warm ambient shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(36, 26, 32, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(36, 26, 32, 0.04)`.
4. **Modal Overlays (Level 3):** Backdrop uses `#241A20` tinted to 40% opacity. Stacking rule: strictly a single overlay level is allowed at any time. Multi-tiered nested dialogs are barred.

## Shapes

The geometric personality balances youth-friendly approachability with intentional editorial rigor using three standardized curvature tiers:

### Curvature Tiers
- **Pills (`999px`):** Step tags, category chips, status badges, and small counter bubbles.
- **Cards & Content Surfaces (`20px`):** All modular white content panels, feature banners, and comparison matrix cards.
- **Form Controls & Interactive Elements (`12px`):** Text input fields, dropdown trigger buttons, checkboxes, radio bounding touch frames, and secondary action wrappers.
- **Hero Containers (`28px`):** Reserved for large top-level onboarding banners.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button:** 48px height, `12px` radius. Background `#6E2035`, text `#FFFFFF`, typography `body-strong`. Hover state `#57172A`. Transitions timed between 120ms and 180ms.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** 48px height, `12px` radius. Background transparent, border `1px solid #8C7D85`, text `#241A20`. Hover background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Keyboard Focus:** Every interactive button and link reveals a crisp `3px solid #6E2035` ring with `3px` offset on `:focus-visible`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Tags:** Height 28px–32px, `999px` full radius. Internal padding: 4px 12px.
- **Domain Indicators:** Tinted with domain colors (e.g., Blush `#F6E6EA` for Self-Reflection) paired with text `#241A20`.
- **Status Badges:** Always combine an icon with text. Success pairs `#E7EEE7` with `#315D46`; attention pairs `#FFF0D5` with `#7A4C15`. Never rely on color alone to indicate progress or status.

### Cards & Structured Containers
- **Standard Card:** Radius `20px`, surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7DEE2`, internal padding `24px`.
- **Domain Feature Card:** Tinted background using the step's pastel token, zero drop-shadow, with `1px solid` border mixed 10% darker than the pastel fill.
- **Asymmetric Composition:** Large headline block paired side-by-side with structured modular metric or checklist cells.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Input & Textarea:** Radius `12px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #8C7D85`, padding 12px 16px. Text `#241A20`, placeholder `#6D6268`. Active focus triggers the primary maroon border and 3px outline.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hit targets minimum `44px x 44px`. Checkbox indicator is a 20px box with `6px` radius; radio is a 20px circle. Selected state fills with `#6E2035` carrying a pure white check/dot icon.

### Iconography
- **Visual Style:** 1.75px–2px stroke outline geometry. No filled shapes except within active selection markers.
- **Standard Sizes:** 20px for inline card metadata; 24px for primary navigation and action headers.
- **Constraint:** Emojis are strictly forbidden as functional UI icons.
